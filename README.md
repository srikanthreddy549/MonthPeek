## What it is

MonthPeek puts the current month in your Mac's menu bar. Click the calendar icon and a compact glass panel opens with today highlighted. Move between months, jump back to today, and close it again without opening a full calendar app.

## Features

- **One click, no window.** A compact panel opens from the menu bar and closes when you click away.
- **Liquid Glass look.** Uses Apple's Liquid Glass on macOS 26, with a frosted material on earlier versions.
- **Today is easy to find.** Today sits in a blue circle. Dates from neighbouring months are faded, and the grid always shows six rows so the panel never changes height.
- **Sundays in red.** The Sunday column and its weekday letter are red.
- **Smooth month navigation.** Round arrow buttons slide between months. A Today button appears only when you have moved away from the current month.
- **Launch at login.** One switch in the panel starts MonthPeek when you sign in.
- **Light and dark.** Follows your Mac's appearance setting.
- **Private and offline.** No accounts, no network use, and it never asks to read your calendar events.

## Install

1. Download **MonthPeek.dmg** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Open the .dmg and drag **MonthPeek** onto the **Applications** shortcut. Then eject the disk.
3. Open **MonthPeek** from Applications. macOS will say it cannot verify the app, because it is not yet notarized by Apple. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the message about MonthPeek, and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.
4. Click the calendar icon in the menu bar and switch on **Launch at login**
